Questions, Answered
What counts as "content published after today"?
Every class, supplement, system, generator, engine, or tool we release on phmurtstudios.com. If we publish it here, Lifetime members have access the day it goes live.
What if the studio shuts down?
If Phmurt Studios ever winds down, we commit to open-sourcing the already-published material and keeping the site accessible for 12 months minimum. You won't lose your characters or campaigns — full data export is built in.
Can I refund?
Yes. 14-day no-questions refund. Email contact@phmurtstudios.com and we'll process it.
How is it different from Pro?
Pro is $5/month. Lifetime is $50 once — so it breaks even at 10 months. You also get the founder badge, the private roadmap channel, access to every future content drop (Pro doesn't include exclusive future content), and immunity from future price changes. Pro is a monthly tool; Lifetime is a bet on the studio.
What if I already have a Pro subscription?
You can upgrade to Lifetime and we'll prorate your current Pro billing cycle. Your Pro sub cancels automatically at checkout and you start Lifetime the same day.
Will Lifetime ever come back after it sells out?
No. The Lifetime Pass is a one-time founder offer capped at 200. When it's gone, Pro and Party Pass are the only ongoing options. We won't re-open it at a higher price, we won't quietly raise the cap, and we won't do a "second wave."
