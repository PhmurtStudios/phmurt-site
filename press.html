Boilerplate Copy

Short — 1 Sentence

Phmurt Studios is an independent tabletop game studio building homebrew classes, adventures, and digital tools for D&D 5e and OGL-compatible systems.

Medium — 1 Paragraph

Phmurt Studios is a small independent tabletop game studio. We make homebrew classes (starting with the Soup Savant), digital tools (a character builder, a campaign tracker, 13 generators, and an expanding compendium of homebrew content), and adventures for D&D 5e and OGL-compatible systems. Every team member runs a home game, and everything we ship is tested at real tables before it reaches the site.

Long — 2 Paragraphs

Phmurt Studios is an independent tabletop game studio founded in 2025. We build homebrew classes, campaign tools, and adventures for D&D 5e and OGL-compatible systems. The studio's public releases include the Soup Savant — a full 20-level homebrew class with three subclasses — the Legendary compendium of homebrew races and subclasses, a 13-generator toolkit covering NPCs, monsters, taverns, treasure, and more, and a character builder supporting both 5e and 3.5e.

Beyond individual tools, Phmurt runs a deep campaign management layer featuring timelines, NPC relationships, faction tracking, kingdom management, downtime systems, and integrated homebrew content. The studio operates on a free-first model — most tools are free, with a Pro tier for cloud sync and unlimited characters, a Party Pass for shared campaigns, and a limited Lifetime option capped at 200 slots.

Flagship Products

Homebrew Class
The Soup Savant

A 20-level D&D 5e homebrew class that channels divine magic through cooking. Three subclasses, custom spell list, and full ingredient and recipe systems. Free.

Compendium
Legendary

A growing, filterable library of homebrew races, subclasses, feats, and magic items — all free and copy-pasteable into real character sheets.

Tools
13 Generators

NPCs, monsters, taverns, encounters, treasure, plot hooks, spells, wild magic, potions, weather, flavor, maps, and loot shops. All free. All work offline.

Tool
Character Builder

Opinionated, readable character creation for both 5e and 3.5e. Saves locally by default, cloud sync with a Pro account.

Campaign Suite
Campaign Engine

Timelines, NPC relationships, factions, quests, downtime, kingdoms, intrigue, hexcrawls, and crafting systems — all integrated.

In Development
The Runewright

Our second full homebrew class. Currently in late-stage playtest. See the roadmap for status.
